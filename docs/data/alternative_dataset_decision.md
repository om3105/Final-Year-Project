# Dataset alternatives if ADNI access is unavailable

**Reviewed 30 September 2026. No alternative dataset has been requested or downloaded.** This is a decision aid, not a silent change to the ADNI-first study. The [primary strategy](dataset_strategy.md) remains in force until an explicit project decision and updated ADR/experiment plan.

| Option | Access path | Fit to paired MRI + clinical disease classification | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MIRIAD (UCL)** | [UCL's public dataset page](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/brain-sciences/ion/research/research-centres/dementia-research-centre/research-clinical-trials/minimal-interval-resonance-imaging-alzheimers-disease-miriad) says users register with its XNAT and accept terms; redistribution to third parties is prohibited without agreement. The linked XNAT timed out during this review, so current portal operation was **not verified**. | 46 AD cases and 23 controls, with 708 repeated T1 scans and age, sex, MMSE/CDR information. There is **no MCI class**. Use one baseline scan per person or group all repeated scans within a partition. MMSE/CDR are potential label proxies, leaving a very small independent tabular set. | **Potential binary pilot after registration and portal verification**, not a replacement for the original CU/MCI/dementia study or evidence for a large clinical Transformer. A 69-person cohort will yield very uncertain held-out estimates. |
| **OASIS-1** | OASIS labels this an **open dataset** but its [access form](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/home/access/) still asks for a detailed research statement, agreement acceptance and an institutional email. | Official [OASIS-1 page](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/home/oasis-1/) describes 416 participants, T1 MRI and demographic/clinical CSV, including 100 older participants clinically diagnosed with very mild to moderate AD. Exact eligible counts and independent input columns need audit. | Best **course-project fallback** for a narrower image-plus-small-tabular experiment if access is granted. Do not promise a three-class MCI study or a useful Clinical Transformer from this release. |
| **OASIS-2** | Also listed as open on the [OASIS form](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/home/access/), with the same applicant requirements. | Official [OASIS-2 page](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/home/oasis-2/) describes 150 older participants and 373 MR sessions, plus demographic data. Longitudinal visits support a different progression question, but the small cohort and repeated scans demand participant-level grouping. | Alternative if a longitudinal study is specifically desired; weaker first choice for a large multimodal Transformer. |
| **OASIS-3** | Reviewed request, institutional email and NITRC account under the [OASIS access process](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/request-access/). | Approximately 1,378 participants and 2,842 MR sessions in the [official overview](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/), with richer clinical/cognitive records. Pairing and labels require release-specific audit. | Stronger scientific substitute for ADNI, but still restricted; not an immediate no-approval shortcut. |
| **OASIS-4** | Reviewed request and NITRC account. | Distinct memory-complaint cohort of 663 in the [official overview](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/); different case mix. | Possible separate study, not an automatic external test for ADNI/OASIS-1 labels. |
| **NACC/SCAN** | [Free worldwide researcher request](https://www.naccdata.org/data-request-process/) and named-user DUA. | Rich longitudinal clinical and structural MRI subset, but [NACC's guide](https://www.naccdata.org/the-nacc-researchers-guide) requires investigator-defined scan/visit matching. Published source totals are not paired eligible counts. | Scientifically strong but more complex access, harmonization and governance. |
| **PEARL-Neuro on OpenNeuro** | Public fMRI/health/risk data, but its [dataset page](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds004796/versions/1.0.9) says the 79 imaged participants are healthy and anatomical MRI requires a separate DUA. | No disease-classification cases in the imaged cohort. | Exclude for this endpoint despite public metadata. |
| **OpenNeuro Synapse cohort, ds007561** | OpenNeuro allows [browser download of public snapshots](https://docs.openneuro.org/user_guide.html). Its [public dataset entry](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds007561/versions/1.0.0) is summarized by OpenNeuro search as UCB-J PET plus T1 MRI across normal, MCI and Alzheimer-dementia diagnoses. | A `participants.tsv` is reported, but the participant count, exact independent clinical columns and version-specific license were **not verified from an accessible dataset detail page**. | Metadata-audit candidate only. Do not promise a useful training sample or select it as primary until these details and image/label matching are verified. |
| **Stanford SPHERE synthetic release** | [Official portal](https://adrc-sphere.stanford.edu/) offers a synthetic cohort without a data-use request, but a DUA still applies. | Synthetic clinical, PET regional intensities and cortical-thickness phenotypes; the described release does not establish downloadable raw T1 volumes. | Useful for tabular pipeline demonstrations, not real-image Alzheimer model validation. |

The [OASIS FAQ](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/home/oasis-resources-and-faq/) recommends OASIS-1/2 for class projects and advises against combining OASIS-1, -2 and -3 because participants can overlap under different identifiers. A public download link does not waive the stated OASIS access terms or authorize third-party cloud storage.

## Recommendation under the user's current access situation

1. **Try ADNI personally or with a project guide** using the [ADNI packet](adni_access_request_packet.md); ADNI has [published an undergraduate application route](https://adni.loni.usc.edu/support/experts-knowledge-base/question/?QID=1853).
2. For a quicker binary MRI pilot, investigate **MIRIAD registration** and its exact terms. Its small sample and absence of MCI require narrower claims and participant-level grouping.
3. If the user has an eligible institutional email, **request OASIS-1** as another course-project fallback through the [official form](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/home/access/). A detailed research statement must cover aims, methods and variables. OASIS staff decide access.
4. If only MIRIAD or OASIS-1 is obtainable, formally **narrow the research question** to baseline MRI plus limited independent demographics/clinical fields. Audit exact fields and label construction first. Do not claim ADNI-equivalent multimodal evidence, MCI classification, early prediction, or a justified Clinical Transformer without data.
5. Request OASIS-3 only if the project guide supports its access and handling requirements. Consider NACC if broader clinical data are needed and the team can manage scan/visit harmonization.
6. Do not train on scraped Kaggle copies of ADNI/OASIS images whose provenance, participant overlap, labels and redistribution rights cannot be verified.

Before any switch, update [dataset strategy](dataset_strategy.md), [data dictionary](data_dictionary.md), [system architecture](../architecture/system_architecture.md), [experiment plan](../ml/experiment_plan.md), ADR, README and CHANGELOG. Restricted data remain outside Git and AI prompts.
