# Research gaps supported by the collection

1. **Added clinical value needs controlled tests.** Models `[1]`, `[2]`, `[4]`, `[17]`, and `[27]` combine image and non-image information, but they use different cohorts and tasks. DeepALZNET's two pathways use *separate* clinical and MRI datasets and cannot quantify paired fusion benefit `[5]`. The project must compare image-only, clinical-only, concatenation, and attention fusion on the **same patients and split**, with confidence intervals. The Three-City study's AUC change of 0.80 to 0.83 after MRI was added shows that incremental benefit can be modest `[30]`.
2. **Independent information matters.** TriFusion's clinical text is MRI-derived `[1]`, while OmniBrain uses independent clinical and gene-expression features alongside MRI-derived radiomics `[2]`. The project will label every feature by origin and test an independent-clinical subset separately. It will not call MRI-derived volume summaries an independent clinical modality.
3. **Cross-cohort generalization is unresolved.** OmniBrain reports a large accuracy drop on MRI-only external ADNI data `[2]`; TriFusion identifies its single-ADNI-cohort limitation `[1]`. Reviews `[6]`, `[32]`, and `[36]` also discuss translation and validation limits. An external cohort is desirable but cannot be claimed until matching labels and access are secured.
4. **Leakage and temporal validity can inflate estimates.** The Parkinson feature-selection experiment `[33]` and same-admission coding study `[35]` demonstrate different leakage mechanisms. NeuroNet-AD explicitly groups its 200 ADNI subjects but includes MMSE/CDR/FAQ metadata, which may be label proxies depending on the registered target and prediction time `[27]`. MRI slices, repeated visits, preprocessing fitting, and target-derived cognitive scores require explicit controls here.
5. **Explanations are incomplete evidence.** OmniBrain uses Grad-CAM/SHAP `[2]`; explanatory methods can visualize model behavior, but these are not clinical or causal proof. Stability and clinician review are separate evaluation questions `[31]`, `[36]`.
6. **Compute-efficient inference is a separate research constraint.** Method papers `[38]`–`[45]` motivate profiling, pruning or quantization candidates, but none establishes that this specific MRI fusion model will run on a particular free host. Benchmark before fixing serving targets.

These are project investigation priorities. None is claimed as a globally unprecedented discovery.
