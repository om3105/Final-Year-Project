## Verified Source Summary

AD-Transformer combines structural MRI, clinical and genetic data from 1,651 ADNI subjects. A Patch-CNN forms image tokens; a linear layer projects non-image tokens; Transformer blocks model joint features. The publisher reports AUC 0.993 for AD diagnosis and 0.845 for MCI conversion prediction.
