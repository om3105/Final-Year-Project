## Verified Source Summary

OmniBrain integrates brain MRI, radiomics, gene expression, and clinical data using a pretrained MRI encoder, FT-Transformer for tabular inputs, cross-attention and modality dropout. It reports 92.2 ± 2.4% accuracy on ANMerge and 70.4 ± 2.7% on an MRI-only ADNI external test. The paper uses Grad-CAM and SHAP.

## Detailed full-text audit

**Paper facts:** Figure 1 and methods describe grey-matter brain MRI encoded by pretrained AnatCL or y-Aware InfoNCE, while radiomics, gene expression and clinical metadata are represented with FT-Transformer. Cross-modal attention fuses available representations; attention masking and modality dropout address missing inputs. The explainability section names Grad-CAM for image representations and SHAP for tabular inputs. The official proceedings abstract reports ANMerge accuracy 92.2 ± 2.4% and external MRI-only ADNI accuracy 70.4 ± 2.7%. [Official paper and PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025W/CVAMD/html/Sharshar_Not_Only_Grey_Matter_OmniBrain_for_Robust_Multimodal_Classification_of_ICCVW_2025_paper.html).

**Cohort denominator:** The dataset section describes ANMerge as a resource of 1,702 participants, but the analyzed set is 319 unique patients and 919 samples. The paper reports 453 participants with MRI in the broader resource. Group-based five-fold validation is described in the training section. These denominators must remain distinct when reproducing the study.

**Author claims:** the model is robust to absent modalities and yields clinically meaningful attribution patterns. These are study claims, not clinical validation.

**Our interpretation:** the external ADNI condition is MRI-only, so it is not an external test of the full paired MRI/clinical model. The cross-cohort accuracy drop deserves close scrutiny. Radiomics is image-derived and should not be counted as independent clinical evidence.

**Proposed adaptation:** begin with MRI plus independently observed clinical fields, use FT-Transformer and cross-attention as comparators, and test missingness, explanation stability and external generalization separately. A Swin encoder and extra multimodal Transformer require measured justification.
