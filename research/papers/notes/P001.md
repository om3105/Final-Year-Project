## Verified Source Summary

TriFusion-ADFormer uses structural MRI, MRI-derived clinical text, and cognitive assessment scores for AD/MCI/CN classification. Swin Transformer V2 encodes MRI, BERT encodes the MRI-derived text, and an MLP encodes cognitive scores. Embeddings are concatenated and passed to feed-forward layers. The authors report accuracy 86.0%, macro AUC 0.93, and F1 0.86 on ADNI.

## Detailed full-text audit

**Paper facts:** Section 3.1 describes ADNI. Section 3.2 says DICOM slices were converted to RGB and resized to 192×192, without skull stripping, registration or bias correction; missing cognitive scores were set to zero before scaling. Section 3.3 and Figure 2 describe Swin V2, BERT for MRI-derived summaries and a two-layer MLP for cognitive scores. Section 3.3.2 explicitly chooses concatenation and feed-forward fusion. Section 3.4 reports an 80/10/10 split and AdamW/cross-entropy. Section 4 reports 86.0% accuracy, 0.93 macro AUROC, 0.86 F1 and subject-level three-fold CV. Section 5 acknowledges that the generated clinical summaries are derived from MRI and that external/prospective validation is absent. [Full paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/artificial-intelligence/articles/10.3389/frai.2026.1849315/full).

**Cohort denominator:** Section 3.1 reports 105 different subjects and 306 scan sessions. These are not 306 independent people.

**Author claims:** the authors attribute performance to multimodal fusion and describe their subject-level CV as evidence of generalizability. That evidence is within ADNI and does not establish external clinical generalization.

**Our interpretation:** the methods text describing 2D RGB slice preprocessing and its later mathematical description of 3D MRI patches are not fully reconciled in the paper. Do not assume a reproducible 3D Swin pipeline from its equations alone. The cognitive scores may overlap with diagnosis criteria, so their use as predictors needs a separate leakage audit. Zero imputation can conflate missingness and a true zero.

**Proposed adaptation:** use an explicit 3D or 2.5D protocol based on available compute, track missingness separately, and compare the paper's concatenation baseline against cross-attention under one participant-level split. Do not copy the reported metrics as expected results.
