# Data directory

Only directory placeholders belong in Git. Do not commit, paste into AI tools, or share restricted participant-level images, clinical tables, split manifests or derived features. Obtain dataset access independently and follow the official DUA and [dataset strategy](../docs/data/dataset_strategy.md).

For the public CMMD cancer candidate, use the [official TCIA collection](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cmmd/) and the [local acquisition/suitability audit](../docs/data/cmmd_download_audit.md). The downloaded clinical workbook, manifests and partial DICOM set are intentionally outside Git. The local image transfer was stopped before completion; do not train from that folder until a full transfer and integrity audit are complete.
